# Privacy Policy — MealRotate

Last updated: 7 August 2026

## Short version

MealRotate does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data outside your device.
There is no account, no server, and no network connection of any kind.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter — recipe names, tags, cooking times, notes, when you last cooked
something, and the generated week plans — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's
on-device database. Your app settings are stored in the system preferences of the app.

This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.

## What the app does not do

- It does not create an account and does not ask for an email address.
- It does not connect to the internet. There is no server behind it.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- It does not share data with third parties, because it does not send data anywhere.

## Exporting your data

The app can export your recipes as a CSV file. Where that file goes is entirely your
choice, made through the standard iOS share sheet — for example to the Files app, to
another app, or to a service you use. Once you hand the file to another app, that app's
own privacy policy applies to it. MealRotate has no involvement after the export.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data. There is no copy anywhere else
for us to delete, because we never received one.

## Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.
