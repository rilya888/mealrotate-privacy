MealRotate

Your recipes, a week, one tap.

Coming soon to the App Store — iPhone, offline

Stop deciding what to cook

MealRotate keeps your own recipes and deals you a week of seven different dinners with one tap. No catalog to browse, no account to create, no subscription.

The rotation is weighted by how long ago you cooked something, so the meals you keep forgetting come back on their own — and the ones you just made stay out of the way. The cooldown adapts to how many recipes you have: a small collection repeats sooner, a large one spreads out.

What it does

  • Shuffle weekOne tap deals a full week of seven different meals.
  • Pin & rerollKeep the days you already planned, reroll the ones you don't like.
  • Mark as cookedThe rotation learns what you actually made, not just what was planned.
  • CSV import & exportBring your existing spreadsheet in, take your recipes back out any time.
  • Fully offlineNo account, no server, no network connection of any kind. Nothing leaves your phone.
